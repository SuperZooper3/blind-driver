THE VIBE

The chase of Subway Surfers. The chaos of Overcooked. The trust of Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes.

01 / Same car. Different problems.

You need each other.

The driver has the wheel. The navigator has the view. A good getaway takes both.

01 / DRIVER“Your left or my left?”

You drive. Mostly by faith.

Accelerate, brake, and steer through a blurred windshield. Listen for the next turn—and trust the person beside you.

WS pedals · AD steer · SPACE handbrake

02 / NAVIGATOR“Left. A little. Straighten!”

You see. Keep talking.

Call out obstacles, choose the route, and watch the police. Unfolding the map covers your view, so pick your moment.

M map · B look back · hold R repair when slow

Talk in person or use your own voice call. Voice chat isn’t built in. Illustrative callouts above; screenshots show an earlier build.

02 / The scenic route has consequences.

A shortcut.
A wrong turn.
One more try.

Read the map before the fork. Take the longer, quieter road or risk the shortcut. Dodge rocks, line up jumps, and wait for the bridge—if the police give you time.

Crashes break parts and slow you down. Repairs take precious seconds. Get caught or miss the road entirely, and it’s time for another ride.

Longer / quieter Shorter / more hazards Dead end / turn around

03 / Pick your crew.

Same panic. Your setup.

Start with a friend, or learn the handling on your own. Use a desktop browser and keep the game tab visible.

SOLO PRACTICE

Beyond the couch / AI experiments

Can two agents
talk their way out?

The same game is a testbed for communication under time pressure. Two AI agents get separate views and control their own inputs while the simulation keeps moving.

Cohere agents exchange directions and replies. The Google harness pairs a Gemini navigator with a silent Decisions driver. A separate solo prototype lets a human drive with AI guidance.

These are developer experiments, separate from the public play button. The harness evaluates and records runs today; reinforcement learning and fine-tuning are the next research step.
