# Future gameplay ideas

Captured on 2026-11-13. These are planning notes only. Do not implement them until
the user requests the relevant work; unresolved details need design discussion.

## Car

- Modular, damageable car parts are implemented (see README, "Car damage").
- Open details: whether to add repairs during a ride, and other damage sources
  such as hard landings beyond the current wheel damage.

## Obstacles and forest

- Static rocks, tree chicanes, ramps, bumps, construction, and lakes are implemented. Future obstacle types remain open.
- Time-sensitive obstacles.
- A buffer zone of trees.
- Open details: obstacle timing, placement, and what the tree buffer should
  separate or protect.

## Why are we running?

- Establish the reason the players are driving/running away and the stakes.
- This remains an open design question; no story or pursuit mechanic is decided.

## Rules and visual effects

- Wrong turn = death is implemented with marked cliffs, physical falls, shared Game Over distance, and driver-controlled restart.
- Driver blur oscillates between strong and weak.
- Additional effects.
- Open details: blur timing and strength, and which other effects to add.
