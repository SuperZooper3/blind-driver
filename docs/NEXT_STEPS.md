# Future gameplay ideas

Captured on 2026-11-13. These are planning notes only. Do not implement them until
the user requests the relevant work; unresolved details need design discussion.

## Car

- Modular car parts.
- Parts can take damage, with associated debuffs.
- Open details: which parts, damage sources, effects, and repair behavior.

## Obstacles and forest

- More obstacles.
- Time-sensitive obstacles.
- A buffer zone of trees.
- Open details: obstacle timing, placement, and what the tree buffer should
  separate or protect.

## Why are we running?

- Establish the reason the players are driving/running away and the stakes.
- This remains an open design question; no story or pursuit mechanic is decided.

## Rules and visual effects

- Wrong turn = death.
- Driver blur oscillates between strong and weak.
- Additional effects.
- Open details: how a wrong turn is identified, how death/restarting works, blur
  timing and strength, and which other effects to add.
